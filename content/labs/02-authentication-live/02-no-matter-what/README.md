# Exercise: No Matter What

**Tools:** SSH

## Goal

Recover a flag that has been split into pieces scattered across the filesystem.

## Background

The flag is not in one file.
Its pieces were all created at about the same time, recently, which is the thread that ties them together.

## Your Task

1. Connect over SSH to the address listed on the CTF platform.
1. Find the recently created pieces and reassemble them in order.

## Check Your Work

The reassembled pieces form a string of the form `SSP{...}`.
Be ready to explain how you found the pieces.
