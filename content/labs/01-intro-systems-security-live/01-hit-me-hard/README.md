# Exercise: Hit Me Hard

**Tools:** SSH

## Goal

Connect to the challenge and read the flag.

## Background

The flag is already readable by your account; the only task is to get onto the machine and find it.

## Your Task

1. Connect over SSH to the address listed for this challenge on the CTF platform.
1. Locate the flag and read it.

## Check Your Work

You should end up with a string of the form `SSP{...}`.
Submit it on the platform.
