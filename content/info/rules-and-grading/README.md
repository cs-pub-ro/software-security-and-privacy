# Rules and Grading

The grade has three components:

| Component | Points |
| --- | --- |
| Session activity | 2 |
| Assignments | 4 |
| Exam | 4 |

Grades are stored in the gradebook on Moodle, linked from the [resources page](../resources/README.md).

## Session Activity

You get session activity points by being actively involved in the lecture and lab sessions.
In a lab, that means working through the tasks and taking your results to the teaching assistant, not the number of flags you capture.

## Assignments

There are three assignments, each worth 1.33 points.
They are described in the [assignments section](../../assignments/README.md).

## Exam

The exam is worth 4 points, and has two parts:

* A quiz of 22 multiple-choice questions, 2 of them bonus, similar to the lecture quizzes.
  A question answered completely correctly is worth 1 point; each incorrect answer loses 0.5.
  It takes 20 minutes, and it is worth 2 points.
* A concept statement of 11 true or false questions, 1 of them bonus.
  Each answer must come with its argumentation.
  It takes 20 minutes, and it is worth 2 points.

## September Exam

In the September exam session, only the exam may be taken again.
The same holds for the June special session for 2nd year master students.
You can take part only once in a given exam session.

## Keeping Grades

You can choose to keep the lab grade (all labs), the assignments grade (all assignments), or both, from a previous academic year.
You cannot keep grades for lecture tests or for the exam.
To keep grades, reply on the respective thread on Moodle.
